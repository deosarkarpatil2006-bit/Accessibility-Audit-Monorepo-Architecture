## Procedure

- [ ] Reload the page and do not use the mouse.
- [ ] Press Tab to move through interactive elements.
- [ ] Confirm the focused element is visibly identifiable.
- [ ] Press Shift+Tab to verify reverse navigation.
- [ ] Press Enter to activate links.
- [ ] Press Space to operate applicable buttons.
- [ ] Verify that focus is not trapped unexpectedly.
- [ ] Check that menus or dialogs can be opened and closed by keyboard.
- [ ] Check that focus is not obscured by sticky or floating content.
- [ ] Record the exact page element and observed behavior for each failure.
